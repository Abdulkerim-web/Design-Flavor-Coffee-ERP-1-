1.CORE DESIGN OBJECTIVE
============================================================
Create a product that feels:
• extremely simple
• exceptionally beautiful
• intelligent
• calm
• premium
• trustworthy
• operational
• precise
• modern
• fast
• professional
The software will be used daily by real employees.
Reduce cognitive load.
Users should not need to understand databases, APIs, inventory algorithms, accounting rules, or technical terminology.
The interface itself should explain what is happening.
Every important screen and component should help answer:
WHAT IS HAPPENING?
WHAT NEEDS MY ATTENTION?
WHAT CAN I DO?
WHY DO I NEED TO DO IT?
WHAT WILL HAPPEN IF I DO IT?
WHO IS RESPONSIBLE?
WHAT IS BLOCKING THE PROCESS?
FUNCTIONAL CLARITY ALWAYS COMES FIRST.
